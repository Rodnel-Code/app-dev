## About the Series

**Black Clover** is a Japanese anime series written by *Yuki Tabata*. It tells the story of two orphan boys, Asta and Yuno, who dream of becoming the Wizard King.

Asta was born without magic in a world where everyone uses magic. Despite this, he continues to work hard and never gives up on his dream.

## Main Characters

- **Asta** – A boy without magic who uses anti-magic.
- **Yuno** – A talented magician and Asta's childhood rival.
- **Noelle Silva** – A member of the Black Bulls who uses water magic.
- **Yami Sukehiro** – The captain of the Black Bulls.
